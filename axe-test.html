UUStreak axe-core accessibility tester

Back to UUStreak front page

Tests one page at a time using axe-core 4.14.0. The page is fetched through public CORS proxies and tested in an isolated frame. Proxy-rendered pages may differ from a normal visit. All WCAG tags available in axe-core are run, including WCAG 2.0 AAA and WCAG 2.2 AA; axe-core does not provide a WCAG 2.2 AAA tag. Automated checks cannot replace manual testing.
